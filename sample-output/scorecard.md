### Themes & Opportunities

**1. Role Specificity — Too General for Specialist AEs**

> *"I'm a Slack AE so some of this is irrelevant"*

> *"Would love to have more role specific onboarding in person."*

**Opportunity:** Explore role-track breakout sessions or a modular curriculum with specialist supplements.

**2. Pacing & Duration — Too Long for Experienced Hires**

> *"I am a boomerang from 16 months ago, and a lot of this information was not necessary for me to have to go over again."*

**Opportunity:** Tiered onboarding tracks (new hire vs. experienced/boomerang) or pre-assessments to skip known content.

**3. Product Knowledge Depth**

> *"More on Salesforce Solutions and Capabilities/Competitive insights"*

**Opportunity:** Expand or add a dedicated product catalog session; connect to specialist SEs for deep dives.

**4. Presentation Instructions Unclear Early**

> *"Would have loved more clarity early in the week about the deal review presentation."*

**Opportunity:** Distribute presentation brief and rubric on Day 1.

**5. Evening Social & Networking Events**

> *"Would love to have evening social happy hours / events for networking."*

**Opportunity:** Add at least one facilitated evening event per in-person week.

**6. More Live AE Panels / Field Stories**

> *"We need more sessions led by AEs for how they do their job."*

**Opportunity:** Expand panel time; bring in 2–3 AEs per cohort for structured storytelling + Q&A.

### Top 3 Recommended Actions

**1. Build a Specialist Track Supplement**
Design modular breakout sessions (½ day) for the most common specialist segments (Slack, MuleSoft, Public Sector). Directly addresses reach — expanding relevance increases enrollment among specialists.

**2. Clarify Deal Review Presentation Brief on Day 1**
Distribute the rubric, slide template, and presentation expectations on the first morning. Add a 10-minute orientation to the final deliverable at program kickoff.

**3. Add One Evening Social Event Per In-Person Week**
A facilitated dinner or social activity on Day 2 or 3 improves cohort bonding and makes the investment in travel more worthwhile for learners coming from out of market.

### Top 3 Recommended Actions

**1. Build a Specialist Track Supplement (same priority as Foundations)**
The signal is even stronger in On-the-Job than in Foundations. A specialist track directly addresses both the content relevance gap and the reach problem.

**2. Resequence ACT Self-Learner Modules as Pre-Work**
Set module due dates tied to the live session where that content is covered. Reduces in-session catch-up and addresses the pacing overload.

**3. Redesign Week 3 Cadence — Protect Final Critical Topics**
Move account planning, Org62 advanced navigation, and forecasting anchoring sessions to Days 1–3. Add a program-close orientation ("what's next") to help learners understand their continued ramp path.
